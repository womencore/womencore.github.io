What is diastasis recti?

Why your belly
hasn't gone back

Diastasis recti is the widening between the two sides of the rectus abdominis. During pregnancy the linea alba, the connective tissue between them, stretches. It is stretched, not torn.

If that tissue doesn't regain tension after birth, force leaks through the middle when you engage your core. The belly can stay rounded even after weight loss, and back or pelvic pain may follow.

More than finger widths

What matters more than the gap is whether your abdomen can take load. Your core works like a pressure box: breath (the lid), pelvic floor (the base) and abdominal wall (the sides) work together.

It is never too late

The idea that you must fix it in the first six months or never is a myth. Women recover years after giving birth. It just doesn't close on its own.

The womencore order

Recovery has an order

  1. BreathGet your ribs and diaphragm moving again
  2. AlignmentAdd options to the posture pregnancy gave you
  3. Deep coreReconnect with transverse abdominis and pelvic floor
  4. MovementManage pressure while lifting your baby or car seat
  5. StrengthReturn to the training you love

Diastasis recti self-check

Answer 7 questions to see where your core is now. We'll send your report to you.

If you notice leaking or heaviness, please see a doctor before exercising. After that, we can plan your training together.

Check with your fingers at home
  1. Lie on your back with knees bent. Place your fingers vertically above and below your belly button, on the midline.
  2. Breathe out and gently lift your head and shoulders.
  3. Feel how many fingers sink in, whether the midline domes up, and whether it feels firm or soft.
How well your abdomen takes load matters more than finger count. This is a guide for exercise, not a medical diagnosis.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does coaching over Zoom really work?

Most clients had the same worry at first. I watch your posture and breathing on screen and adjust each movement in real time, then give feedback on your exercise log and videos between sessions.

I live outside Korea. Can I join?

Yes. Moms in the US, UK, Germany and Australia are coaching with womencore. Sessions usually run in the Korean morning (evening in the US), and times can be adjusted.

It has been years since I gave birth. Is it too late?

It is never too late to recover. Diastasis recti just does not close on its own.

Will my diastasis close completely in 8 weeks?

Not every gap reaches 0 cm in 8 weeks. The goal is not a number but the ability to control the pressure in your core yourself.

Is the Core Assessment Session a workout?

No. It is a 45-minute Zoom consultation to look at how your body has changed and suggest a direction. There is no obligation to join a program.

I leak or feel heaviness when I cough or jump.

Leaking, heaviness or a bulge you can feel should be checked by a doctor before exercise. After that, we can plan your training together.
